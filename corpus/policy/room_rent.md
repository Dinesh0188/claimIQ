# Policy wording — Room rent, ICU and the proportionate deduction

> SYNTHETIC POLICY WORDING. Written for this project to exercise the retrieval and
> reasoning path. It is representative of common Indian indemnity health policies but
> is not any real insurer's contract.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-ROOM-001 | topic: room_rent_cap -->
### Room rent eligibility
The Company shall be liable for room, boarding and nursing expenses up to the Room Rent
Limit specified in the Policy Schedule, expressed either as an absolute amount per day or
as a percentage of the Sum Insured per day. Where the Insured Person occupies a room whose
tariff exceeds this limit, the excess room charge is not payable and is borne by the
Insured Person.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-ROOM-002 | topic: proportionate_deduction -->
### Proportionate deduction on associated charges
Where the Insured Person is admitted to a room category whose rent exceeds the eligible
Room Rent Limit, all Associated Medical Expenses shall be payable in the same proportion
that the eligible room rent bears to the room rent actually charged.

Illustration: if the eligible limit is 50% of the room rent actually incurred, then only
50% of the Associated Medical Expenses shall be payable. This deduction is applied in
addition to the disallowance of the room rent excess itself, and both are borne by the
Insured Person, not by the Hospital.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-ROOM-003 | topic: associated_charges_definition -->
### Definition of Associated Medical Expenses
Associated Medical Expenses means expenses incurred during hospitalisation that vary with
the room category occupied. These customarily include nursing charges, operation theatre
charges, surgeon and anaesthetist professional fees, and consultant visit charges.

Associated Medical Expenses customarily exclude the cost of pharmacy and consumables, the
cost of implants and medical devices, and the cost of diagnostics and investigations, since
these do not vary with room category.

**The precise composition of this list varies between insurers.** Some insurers include
diagnostics; some exclude consultant visits. A pre-submission estimate should therefore be
expressed as a range across plausible interpretations rather than as a single figure.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-ROOM-004 | topic: icu_cap -->
### Intensive care unit charges
Where the Policy Schedule specifies a separate ICU Limit, charges for intensive care shall
be assessed against that limit rather than the general Room Rent Limit. The proportionate
deduction applies on the same basis if the ICU tariff exceeds the ICU Limit.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-ROOM-005 | topic: proportionate_exemption -->
### When the proportionate deduction does not apply
No proportionate deduction shall be applied where the Policy Schedule records that the
room rent limit is waived, where the Insured Person occupies the lowest room category
offered by the Hospital, or where the hospitalisation is for a procedure reimbursed on a
fixed package basis.
