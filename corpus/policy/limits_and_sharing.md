### Procedure sub-limits
Certain named procedures are payable only up to the monetary sub-limit stated against them
in the Policy Schedule, irrespective of the actual expense incurred or the Sum Insured
available. Where a sub-limit applies, it is applied to the admissible amount for that
procedure after item-level disallowances and any room-rent-related deduction.

Commonly sub-limited procedures include cataract surgery, hernia repair, hysterectomy,
joint replacement and treatment of specified ailments.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-LIM-002 | topic: copay -->
### Co-payment
Where a Co-payment percentage is stated in the Policy Schedule, the Insured Person shall
bear that percentage of each admissible claim amount. Co-payment is applied to the amount
remaining after all disallowances and sub-limits, and before the balance Sum Insured cap.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-LIM-003 | topic: deductible -->
### Deductible
Where a Deductible is stated, the Company is liable only for the amount by which the
admissible claim exceeds the Deductible. The Deductible is applied per policy year unless
the Schedule states otherwise.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-LIM-004 | topic: sum_insured -->
### Sum insured and balance sum insured
The Company's total liability in a policy year shall not exceed the Sum Insured. Where
earlier claims have been paid in the same policy year, liability is limited to the balance
Sum Insured then available.

<!-- chunk_id: POL-LIM-005 | topic: deduction_order -->
### Order of application
Unless the Schedule provides otherwise, deductions are applied in the following sequence:
item-level disallowances; room rent excess and proportionate deduction; procedure
sub-limit; deductible; co-payment; balance sum insured cap.

**The sequence is insurer-variable** and materially changes the settled amount, because a
percentage applied before a cap yields a different result than one applied after.
