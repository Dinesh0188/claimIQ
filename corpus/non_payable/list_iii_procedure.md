Theatre and procedure consumables that are deemed included in the surgical or procedure
package rate. Billing them separately gets them deducted and the hospital absorbs the loss.

Correct remediation: fold into the procedure/package rate.

<!-- chunk_id: L3-001 | list: LIST_III_PROCEDURE | bearer: HOSPITAL -->
### Surgical drapes
Aliases: drape, drapes, surgical drape, OT drape, sterile drape, eye drape, disposable drape
Theatre draping is part of performing the procedure.
