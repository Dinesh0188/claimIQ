# List IV — Subsumed into the COST OF TREATMENT (borne by the HOSPITAL)

> UNVERIFIED SNAPSHOT. Compiled from publicly circulated non-payable item lists.
> Not checked against any current IRDAI circular. Treat as a dated approximation.

Items deemed included in the overall cost of treatment. Like Lists II and III, billing them
separately produces a deduction the hospital absorbs.

Correct remediation: fold into the treatment cost.

<!-- chunk_id: L4-001 | list: LIST_IV_TREATMENT | bearer: HOSPITAL -->
### Admission and registration charges
Aliases: admission charges, registration charges, registration fee, admission fee, enrolment charges, regn charges, regn chrg
Charging for the act of admitting a patient. Included in the cost of treatment.

<!-- chunk_id: L4-002 | list: LIST_IV_TREATMENT | bearer: HOSPITAL -->
### Medical records charges
Aliases: medical records, medical record charges, MRD charges, file charges, case sheet charges
Maintaining the record is part of treating the patient.

<!-- chunk_id: L4-003 | list: LIST_IV_TREATMENT | bearer: HOSPITAL -->
### Service charges and surcharges
Aliases: service charges, surcharge, service tax component, handling charges, establishment charges
Generic uplift applied on top of the bill.

<!-- chunk_id: L4-004 | list: LIST_IV_TREATMENT | bearer: HOSPITAL -->
### Hospitalisation purely for evaluation or diagnosis
Aliases: evaluation charges, diagnostic admission, observation charges, work-up charges
Admission solely to investigate, with no active line of treatment, is not a payable
hospitalisation.

### Urine container and urine bag
Aliases: urine container, urine bag, urobag, urine collection bag, urometer

### Blood reservation and ante-natal booking charges
