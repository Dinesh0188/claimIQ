# List I — Optional items (borne by the PATIENT)

> UNVERIFIED SNAPSHOT. Compiled from publicly circulated non-payable item lists.
> Not checked against any current IRDAI circular. Treat as a dated approximation.

These are personal, convenience or comfort items. They are legitimately non-payable and
the correct remediation is to **tell the patient at admission and take a signed
acknowledgement** — not to absorb them.

<!-- chunk_id: L1-001 | list: LIST_I_OPTIONAL | bearer: PATIENT -->
### Baby food
Aliases: baby food, infant formula, cerelac, baby milk, infant milk powder, formula feed
Food for a newborn or infant accompanying the patient. Personal consumption item, not part
of the insured person's treatment. Bill directly to the patient.

<!-- chunk_id: L1-002 | list: LIST_I_OPTIONAL | bearer: PATIENT -->
### Baby utility charges
Aliases: baby utilities, baby utility charges, baby kit, newborn kit
Utility items supplied for a newborn's convenience rather than medical care.

<!-- chunk_id: L1-003 | list: LIST_I_OPTIONAL | bearer: PATIENT -->
### Beauty services
Aliases: beauty services, salon charges, haircut, shaving charges, cosmetic services
Grooming and cosmetic services. Never payable under a hospitalisation claim.

<!-- chunk_id: L1-004 | list: LIST_I_OPTIONAL | bearer: PATIENT -->
### Belts and braces
Aliases: belt, braces, abdominal belt, lumbo sacral belt, LS belt, abdominal binder
Supportive appliances issued for use after discharge. Patient-borne unless the policy
carries a specific appliance benefit.

<!-- chunk_id: L1-009 | list: LIST_I_OPTIONAL | bearer: PATIENT -->
### Food charges other than the patient's diet
Aliases: attendant food, attendant food charges, food charges, visitor food, guest meals, extra diet
Meals supplied to attendants, relatives or visitors. The patient's own prescribed hospital
diet is payable; everything served to anyone else is not. This is one of the most commonly
billed List I items.
